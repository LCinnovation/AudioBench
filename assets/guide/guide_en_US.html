AudioBench — Beginner's Guide

Follow along; it takes about 10 minutes. Every step tells you where to click, what you will see and what to do if it does not work.

0. Know the window

1. Import a song

How

  1. Find your song in Explorer (.mp3, .wav, .flac are fine).
  2. Drag it onto the dark area and let go.
  3. Or press Ctrl+I and pick the file.

What you will see

A coloured bar (a clip) appears, and a new column with the song name (a track). The wavy line inside is the waveform.

If it does not work

  • Make sure the file ends with one of the supported extensions; convert .ape or .dsf to mp3 first.
  • "Cannot decode" usually means the file itself is broken — try playing it elsewhere.

2. Play and loop

  1. Space starts and stops playback; Home jumps back to the start.
  2. To practise one part: hold Shift and drag a short range on the ruler. It turns blue and that range repeats.
  3. Double-click the ruler to clear the loop.
  4. The slanted-line button toggles the metronome.

No sound? Check the M (mute) button on the track first, then the device shown in the bottom-right corner.

3. Edit a clip

  1. Click a bar to select it — the row of controls above becomes active.
  2. Drag the middle to move it; drag the left or right edge to trim it.
  3. Drag the two small corners on the top edge to fade in and out.
  4. S splits at the playhead, Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates.
  5. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes (up to 200 steps).

4. Slow down or change key

  1. Select a clip.
  2. Speed 50–200: below 100 is slower, above is faster — pitch does not change.
  3. Pitch −12…+12 semitones — speed does not change.
  4. The 1× button resets the speed to 100%.

5. Separate vocals from the backing

  1. Click the track you want to separate.
  2. Click the person icon at the far right of the top bar.
  3. Choose a model: 4 stems · high quality (recommended) or 6 stems if you also need guitar and piano.
  4. Pick the stems, then press Start.
  5. When it finishes, press Create tracks. The source track is muted automatically.

A 3-minute song takes roughly 1 minute; you can cancel at any time and nothing is left behind.

6. Export an mp3

  1. Press Ctrl+E.
  2. Pick a format: MP3 320 (usual), WAV (lossless, large), FLAC (lossless, medium).
  3. Pick the scope: whole mix or the selected track.
  4. Choose a folder and press Start. The folder opens with the file selected.

Seeing "clipping detected"? Pull the master fader down a little and export again.

7. Save and recover

Ctrl+S saves the project (.abproj). A draft is autosaved every 30 seconds; if AudioBench closes unexpectedly it offers to restore it next time.

9. Jargon cheat sheet

TermPlain meaning
TrackOne lane of the timeline
ClipOne piece of audio inside a lane
PlayheadThe red vertical line showing the current position
FaderA volume slider
Mute / SoloM silences this track; S silences every other track
GainVolume, measured in dB
Fade in / outGradually louder at the start / softer at the end
PitchKey change, measured in semitones
StemsThe separated parts of a song
ClippingToo loud — the waveform is cut flat and sounds distorted
